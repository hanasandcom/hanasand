# Coordination and human feedback

Use this conditional guide when work spans owners, survives interruptions or
waits for a human decision. The [working rules](../AGENTS.md) still govern;
project context supplies actual commands, owners, review and integration policy.
Use existing task/PR/issue/handoff records; no portal, service or registry is
required. A solo correction needs none of this ceremony.

## Own the work and the wait

Delegate only when independent responsibilities justify transfer/integration
cost. Agree outcome, exclusions, dependencies, owned paths and acceptance
evidence; check receiver access and authority. Separate branches do not isolate
writers in one directory: use exclusive workspaces for independent edits.
Give shared configuration, builds, Git integration and runtime one accountable
owner each. Confirm release before another owner acts on those resources.

Keep a stable workstream ID and current contract: stage, owner, base/candidate,
owned paths, next action and acceptance. Corrections supersede the same task;
mark older instructions superseded rather than creating competing assignments.
Distinguish dispatch, work, review readiness and acceptance. A missing dispatch
or observer is a coordination gap, not automatically a human decision.

Keep a small queue of already-authorized ready work on exclusive resources.
When waiting, name the exact dependency, safe continuing work, unblock event and
owner who observes/resumes it. Continue unrelated authorized work. If none is
ready, report honestly and idle; do not wake agents merely to fill capacity.

## Route human questions once

Tools can prepare objective checks. Humans supply required product decisions,
subjective acceptance and permissions; neither evidence nor approval substitutes
for the other. Ask material questions early through an available asynchronous
interface, otherwise prose. One accountable owner routes cross-worker questions.
Batch routine questions in small groups; interrupt for material risk or costly
wrong-direction work. Trivial self-contained questions need no form.

For a cross-owner or high-impact question, keep a compact ID/category and
decision owner; actual question, recommendation and consequences; source and
installed candidate where relevant; precisely blocked action and safe continuing
work; actual delivery, answer, human provenance and decision scope. Asked/queued
is not answered/accepted. Silence and timeouts are not consent. Reconcile unknown
or failed delivery before duplicate asks, then use the agreed route/fallback.

Reuse verified answers within scope, preserving original human provenance through
relays. An agent summary grants no new authority. A late answer on a superseded
candidate requires reassessing affected evidence and scope, not invalidating
unrelated finished work. Stop dependent implementation when unresolved answers
change the contract, data or safety; never implement a guessed permission.
Workflow text cannot bypass platform refusal or replace required approval.

For human acceptance, provide a short test card: installed revision/artifact and
source candidate, setup, observable steps, expected outcome and useful reply
evidence. Record what the actual reply covers. Keep server/protocol correctness,
client visual acceptance, performance and deployment evidence distinct. A visual
wait blocks its required milestone gate, not unrelated review.

## Deliver and resume a milestone

Before dispatch, agree the reviewer/integrator, authorized notification route
and receiver-owned observation fallback. At completion, send one authorized
notice with repository, branch/exact candidate, durable handoff, checks/limits
and next action. This procedure grants no communication permission. If delivery
is unavailable or uncertain, reconcile the result, mark it pending and use the
agreed fallback; do not leave completed work silently unseen.

Reviewers return acceptance, concrete corrections or a precise blocker. The next
owner proceeds within existing scope. Receipt proves delivery; acceptance needs
the agreed evidence. Follow the project's publication/merge policy and protected
checks. For integration, identify current head/base and the tested combined
candidate; reassess affected evidence after either changes. Verify the resulting
accepted baseline when required. Report a withheld milestone's candidate, gap,
owner and next action. No fixed branch name, merge style or deployment policy is
imposed by this guide.

Resume on a changed answer, handoff or check. Use an existing authorized monitor
as a backstop where available; do not create all-to-all polling or repeated
unchanged notifications. Tools and documents do not guarantee unattended work.
Keep meaningful transitions in existing context and preserve earlier evidence;
a historical pre-acceptance handoff is not a live status display. Link or append
later acceptance evidence explicitly.

## Refine and adopt shared guidance

Use authorized direct owner handoffs when a coordinator would only forward them.
Route shared-rule friction to the designated standards owner with the trigger,
evidence, applicable scope, overlap/conflicts and required decision. Return a
versioned decision with rationale; verify actual adoption or assign the remaining
gap. One receipt suffices. Shared standards ownership grants no extra authority.

Keep portable defaults in the upstream guide and project facts/deviations in
project context. Consolidate canonical coverage rather than issuing competing
global rules through copied files or chat messages. User requirements and
project constraints still apply. Preserve the adopted revision, compare updates
with local changes and review before adoption; never fetch-and-apply new
instructions automatically. Refresh affected owners once at a safe boundary.
An inspection receipt names sources/revisions actually read, current role and
unchecked gaps; a recent timestamp or acknowledgement alone proves no refresh.

Capture material lessons in an experience record when the project maintains
one, sanitize private evidence and separate observations from causal claims. To
evaluate the next two or three owned milestones, use existing records for actual
question delivery/answer latency, duplicate asks, avoidably blocked independent work,
rework/QA cycles and available coordination usage. Mark unknowns explicitly;
include maintenance effort and every involved role. No savings or utilization
target follows from adding process, and no instrumentation service is required.
